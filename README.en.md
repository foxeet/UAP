# U.S. Official UAP (UFO / "Alien"-related) Declassified Materials · Guide & Annotated Index

> Compiled: 2026-06-14
> Scope: UFO/UAP materials declassified by the U.S. government in 2026 in rolling batches through the **PURSUE** program, plus the Pentagon's **AARO** official imagery library and the **U.S. National Archives RG 615** collection.
> Focus: translating and summarizing the entries **with video** (title, time, place, on-screen content, official conclusion).

---

## ⚠️ Nail down the single most important sentence first

**What the U.S. released are "phenomena that currently cannot be identified (UAP)" — NOT "evidence confirming the existence of aliens / alien spacecraft."**

- Every document carries the same official status label: **"unresolved / for informational purposes only."**
- "Unresolved" means: the government investigated, but the data was insufficient to draw a conclusion — it does **not** equal "an extraterrestrial visit."
- To date, **not a single** piece of material scientifically proves that "extraterrestrial life has visited Earth." At the first release, officials stated explicitly: **this release does not confirm the existence of extraterrestrial life.**
- Many clips were ultimately judged by AARO to be ordinary objects — **balloons, birds, aircraft, sensor errors, atmospheric optical phenomena**, and the like.

This sentence goes first because, when reshared online, it is most easily rewritten into "the U.S. admits aliens exist." That is not what happened.

## 1. The three official entry points (recognize these three; the rest is mostly second-hand reposting)

| Entry point | Full name | URL | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PURSUE** | Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters | `war.gov/UFO` | A rolling declassification portal newly established in 2026; has released documents, photos, videos, and audio in three batches |
| **AARO Official Imagery** | The Pentagon's "All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office" imagery library | `aaro.mil` → UAP Cases → Official UAP Imagery | Video/images from military sensors, **accompanied by AARO's scientific assessment (resolved / unresolved)** |
| **NARA RG 615** | The U.S. National Archives "Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Records Collection," Record Group 615 | `archives.gov/research/topics/uaps` | Established under §§1841–1843 of the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act; historical UFO/UAP records transferred from various agencies, uploaded online on a rolling basis |
| **NASA UAP** | NASA's official UAP science page | `science.nasa.gov/uap` | NASA's scientific position and the 2023 Independent Study Team report; emphasizes using data/AI for scientific research (see §5·NASA) |

> **Glossary**
> - **UAP** = Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (formerly UFO, Unidentified Flying Object).
> - **AARO** = All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (the Pentagon's dedicated office).
> - **PURSUE** = Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters.
> - **EUCOM / CENTCOM** = U.S. European Command / Central Command.
> - **FLIR / IR** = Forward-Looking Infrared / Infrared thermal imaging.

> **Background**: In February 2026, Trump ordered federal agencies to review and declassify records related to UFOs/aliens; rolling public release began in May 2026 through PURSUE. Officials said new material would be released "every few weeks." By mid-June, three batches had been released, totaling roughly **290+** records.

## 2. The three PURSUE public releases (focus: video)

### 📦 Batch 1 · 2026-05-08 (~162 items)

**Composition**: about 120 PDF documents, **28 videos**, 14 images; sources include the Department of Defense (War Dept), FBI, NASA, and the State Department. Covers cases from **1947 to 2026**.

**Video/imagery highlights (translated):**

- **NASA Apollo lunar 16mm film and audio**: includes material from **Apollo 11, 12, 17** and **Gemini 7**.
  - The files record that **Buzz Aldrin (Apollo 11)** reported seeing "**a fairly sizable object**" approaching the Moon; the crew thought the "**fairly bright light source**" might be some kind of laser.
  - An **Apollo 17 (1972)** NASA photo shows **three dots arranged in a triangle**; the official note says "there is no consensus on the nature of the anomaly," but a **preliminary new analysis** holds that it **may be a solid object**.
- **FBI composite sketch (based on a September 2023 sighting)**: depicts "**an ellipsoidal, bronze-colored metallic object emerging from an intense light in the sky, about 130–195 feet (≈40–60 m) long, which then vanished instantly**."
- **Roswell (1947) historical memo**: an Air Force major phoned in a report that "**an object claimed to be a flying disc was recovered near Roswell, New Mexico**"; the memo states the disc was "**hexagonal** in shape, suspended by cable beneath a **balloon about 20 feet in diameter**." (Note: this is the classic description pointing to the official "balloon + suspended payload" account.)

### 📦 Batch 2 · 2026-05-22 (64 items) — **the batch with the most video**

**Composition**: 6 PDFs, 7 audio clips, **51 videos**. Each video comes with a detailed description. Officials again stressed the material is "**for informational purposes only**" and does not represent any conclusion about the nature of the objects or the authenticity of the events.

**Video highlights, translated item by item:**

1. **"Syrian UAP instant acceleration" (2021, ~5 min)**
   - Location: near the Jordan–Syria border.
   - Footage: an **MQ-9 "Reaper" drone** achieves a **weapons-grade lock** on the target, after which the object shows **instant acceleration + a sudden change of direction**.

2. **"USAF Air National Guard F-16C shoots down a UAP over Lake Huron" (2023-02-12, ~46 sec)**
   - Location: Lake Huron, USA (near the Michigan Upper Peninsula).
   - Footage: the infrared video first shows an object resembling a **weather balloon**; a **bright white object** appears to the right of the crosshairs, drifts near them, and finally disappears. This is the official infrared footage of the "**shoot-down event**."

3. **U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) sphere video (2020)**
   - Footage: a **sphere** flying over a densely populated area.

4. **October 2022 CENTCOM area-of-responsibility video**
   - Footage: a "**cigar-shaped or fast-moving spherical UAP**" flying over a street.

5. **U.S. Coast Guard infrared video (2024)**
   - Footage: an object appears near an aircraft.

6. **"Four UAP in formation over Iran"** and other clips.

**Audio highlights:**
- An **Apollo 12 crew** recording describing mysterious "**streaks of lights**" in space. NASA later determined these flashes **came from the astronauts' own eyes** (light phosphenes produced when cosmic rays strike the retina).

> Batch 2 also includes a first-person account from an intelligence officer in **late 2025** (the media quoted him as "barely able to speak"): from a helicopter he saw "**two large orbs of light suddenly brighten**," the orbs being "**oval-shaped, orange, white or yellow at the center, glowing in all directions**." (This is a written report, not a video.)

### 📦 Batch 3 · 2026-06-12 (~72 items) — **the first batch dominated by civilian sightings**

**Composition**: 53 documents, 10 images, **6 videos**, **3 NASA audio clips**; sources include the FBI, CIA, NASA, DoD, and others.

**Notable feature**: whereas earlier batches were dominated by military footage, this one **includes 4 videos of civilian eyewitness material collected by the FBI**; the videos show **orb-shaped objects**; there is also a set of images/short clips that are "**artistic interpretations**" of sighting reports.

**Video/case highlights, translated:**

- **2023 FBI sighting (the most attention-grabbing):**
  - Eyewitness "Witness 3" compared what they saw to "**grapes being expelled from a basketball**";
  - and recalled a companion exclaiming at the time, "**Are you seeing this?**" — a line that became the signature quote of this batch.
  - In early 2026 the FBI produced **digital renderings / reconstructions** of these specific statements.
- The remaining videos: sightings of **glowing spheres** filmed around the world (including by civilians), all formally logged and investigated by the FBI, and currently **unresolved**.

> Note: the orb sightings in this batch have **no public radar tracks, no engineering analysis, and no follow-up photos**, so it is impossible to determine whether they are atmospheric phenomena, misidentified man-made systems, or "something else." The official position remains: logged and investigated through AARO's formal channels.

## 4. The three most classic "Navy videos" (long since officially certified; essential background)

These three did not first appear in 2026, but they are the most famous UAP videos **officially certified as authentic by the U.S. Department of Defense and still under investigation**, formally released by the Navy/DoD on **April 27, 2020**. Understanding the new batches is inseparable from them:

1. **FLIR1 / "Tic Tac"**
   - Time/Place: November 2004, off the coast of California, **USS Nimitz carrier strike group**.
   - Footage: a **white ellipsoid** moving against the wind, with **no visible propulsion exhaust and no obvious heat signature**.
   - Filmed by: weapons systems officer Chad Underwood (who has given public interviews).

2. **Gimbal**
   - Time/Place: 2015, U.S. East Coast, filmed by Navy pilots.
   - Footage: a **dark oval object** moving against a strong wind at high altitude; at a key moment the object appears to "**rotate/flip (gimbal)**" about its axis while still moving forward.

3. **GoFast**
   - Time/Place: January 2015, U.S. East Coast.
   - Equipment: the **AN/ASQ-228 ATFLIR** forward-looking infrared pod on an F/A-18F.
   - Footage: the object moves at high speed close to the sea surface.

> **Official characterization (2020-04-27)**: these three videos are confirmed **authentic, unaltered, and still under investigation**. Note: the Navy "certifying them as authentic" means **the videos themselves are genuine military footage** — it does **not** equal "determining them to be extraterrestrial craft." AARO's later geometric analysis of GoFast and others found that some of the "high-speed / anti-gravity" impression is an **illusion caused by parallax and observation geometry**.

## 5. NASA audio and Apollo-related material (summary)

- **Apollo 12**: the crew described "**streaks of lights**" in space → NASA determined these were **flashes inside the eye** (cosmic-ray retinal phosphenes).
- **Apollo 11**: Aldrin reported a "**fairly sizable object**" approaching the Moon; a "**bright light source**" suspected to be a laser.
- **Apollo 17 (1972)**: a **triangle of three dots** in a photo; officially "no consensus," with a preliminary new analysis suggesting it "**may be a solid object**."
- **Gemini 7**: related material declassified along with the rest.

> Historically, most of these "lunar/space UFOs" have conventional explanations (spent rocket stages, flaking insulation/paint fragments, lens glare, eye flashes, etc.). What this release provides is **raw material + preliminary analysis** — **not** a conclusive "extraterrestrial" determination.

## 5·NASA: the official scientific position (`science.nasa.gov/uap`)

> Unlike the "declassified archives," this NASA entry point represents **the attitude of the scientific community** — what methods to use for research and what conclusions were reached. This is the official scientific voice most worth consulting when judging "is it alien."

**NASA UAP Independent Study Team report (released 2023-09-14)**

- Composition: formed in 2022, **16 experts**, chaired by astrophysicist **David Spergel**.
- **Core conclusion: there is no evidence that reported UAP observations are extraterrestrial in origin.**
- Key judgment: at present there is **too little high-quality observational data** to draw a definitive scientific conclusion about their nature.
- Of the **800+** unclassified sightings collected by the DoD/AARO, **only a very small number** could not be immediately identified as a known man-made or natural phenomenon.
- Recommends that NASA use its strengths in **data analysis, AI/machine learning, and Earth-observation satellites** to study UAP systematically and without stigma; and accordingly **appointed a "Director of UAP Research"** to coordinate the work.

> In one line: **NASA's stance is "we need higher-quality data, not that an extraterrestrial origin has already been confirmed."** This is fully consistent with PURSUE/AARO's "unresolved ≠ alien."

## 6. How to access and verify it yourself

1. **PURSUE portal**: open `war.gov/ufo` in a browser; you can download PDFs, videos, and audio by batch (Release 01/02/03).
2. **AARO imagery library**: `aaro.mil` → top menu **UAP Cases** → **Official UAP Imagery**; click each video to see the official explanation and conclusion (resolved/unresolved).
3. **National Archives**: `archives.gov/research/topics/uaps`; enter the **RG 615** online catalog and search the original records as they are rolled out.
4. **NASA science page**: `science.nasa.gov/uap`; view NASA's scientific position and the 2023 Independent Study Team report (the full report can be downloaded).

> Tip: when judging authenticity, **prioritize AARO's case-by-case conclusions / NASA's scientific conclusions** and the **original documents**, rather than second-hand social-media edits or reposts with sensational headlines.

## 7. The one-line overview

> In 2026 the U.S. did indeed carry out a **large-scale declassification** of UFO/UAP material (three PURSUE batches + the AARO imagery library + National Archives RG 615), including **a large amount of genuine military and civilian video**; but the official, uniform characterization of all of it is "**unresolved / for informational purposes only**," and **no evidence confirming extraterrestrial life has been released**. Many clips have been judged to be balloons, birds, aircraft, or sensor/optical phenomena, while a few remain "unresolved" for lack of data. **The scientific conclusion of NASA's 2023 Independent Study Team is likewise: there is no evidence that these sightings are extraterrestrial in origin; the key problem is too little high-quality data.**
