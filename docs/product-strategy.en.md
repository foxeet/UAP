# Product Strategy: A "Science-Grounded Contact-Preparedness" Site for "What Do Earthlings Do If We Meet Aliens in 2027"

> Document type: product/business blueprint (v1)
> Compiled: 2026-06-14
> In one line: take the least serious topic on the entire internet and make it **the most serious, most credible, and most actionable** one.

---

## 0. The important responsible framing (set the rules first, then talk business)

1. **"2027" is a scenario exercise, not a prophecy.** The date comes from a February 2023 podcast claim by former CIA officer John Ramirez, later amplified by remote-viewing, Pleiadian, and similar circles — it is **hearsay/speculation, not a scientific conclusion**. Critics compare it to Y2K and the 2012 Mayan apocalypse. We **use it as a hook to attract traffic, but always clearly label it as hearsay** — which is precisely the product's selling point (see "rumor vs. evidence" rating).
2. **Science first; no fear-mongering, no pseudoscience, no fraudulent monetization.** We don't sell things like "alien refuge insurance" or "anti-abduction amulets." What we sell is **understanding, tools, and community.**
3. **Honestly distinguish three things**: ① established facts (UAP declassification, SETI protocols); ② serious discussion within the scientific community (post-detection protocols, the METI debate); ③ popular hearsay (2027, conspiracy theories). On the site, the three are **always visually layered, never blurred together.**

> This section is not a disclaimer but a **moat**: in a field that is 99% clickbait and grift, "credibility" itself is the scarcest, most valuable asset.

## 1. Positioning and naming

**One-line positioning**: Earth's "contact response" education and preparedness platform — using real science to answer "if it really happens, what should we (as individuals/society/humanity) do."

**Candidate site names (CN/EN)**:
| Chinese | English | Tone |

## 2. Why now (market opportunity)

- **Supply-side explosion from officials**: the U.S. 2026 PURSUE three-batch declassification + the AARO imagery library + NASA's 2023 Independent Study report have turned UAP from a "fringe topic" into a "government-stamped public issue." The topic now has **officially-backed legitimacy.**
- **Heated demand side**: polls such as Gallup show a substantial share of the public believes in UFOs/aliens; prediction markets like Kalshi even open lines on "will the U.S. confirm the existence of aliens" — indicating a high-intent crowd willing to "place bets."
- **Maturing science side**: SETI post-detection protocols are being updated in 2022–2025, St Andrews has established a **Post-Detection Hub**, and "how humanity should respond" has, for the first time, a **serious academic scaffold** to cite.
- **A gap on the competitive side**: existing players each occupy a corner, but **no one credibly occupies the "what should we do" layer** (see §4).

> Conclusion: topic heat, official legitimacy, scientific seriousness, and a competitive gap are **rarely all present at once**. The window is 2026–2028.

## 3. Comparison of four product forms (diverge first, then converge)

| Form | Core asset | Credibility | Demand/Traffic | Monetization potential | Sustainability | Moat | Ethical risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Scientific explanation / media credibility | content + authority | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★★ | medium (brand) | low |
| B Citizen science + community preparedness | community + data | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★★★ | high (data network effects) | medium (data privacy) |
| C Contact-response protocol tool / scenario-simulation SaaS | product + tools | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★★ | medium-high (product) | medium (over-promising) |
| D Media brand / IP | brand + content IP | ★★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★ | low→medium | medium (slide into entertainment) |

**Recommendation: A (credibility) as the trunk + C (protocol tool) as the differentiating signature + D (media) as the traffic engine + B (community/data) as the long-term moat.** Order matters: **first use D to pull traffic and A to build trust, then use C/B to retain and monetize.**

Rationale: A provides "why trust me," C provides "what I can do for you (that others can't)," D solves cold-start acquisition, and B provides network effects that can't be copied. Betting on any single one is unstable: pure A is hard to monetize, pure D easily slides into cheap entertainment, pure B gets stuck like Enigma at "free and unsure how to charge," and pure C easily over-promises and turns into selling anxiety.

## 4. Competitive differentiation: which word do we own

- **Enigma Labs** = "**report** sightings" (50k+ reports, VC-backed, but **free/ad-free, monetization unproven**).
- **Galileo Project / Avi Loeb** = "**research**" (Harvard, philanthropic funding, proposing a billion-dollar "UAP Manhattan Project").
- **MUFON / The Debrief / Coast to Coast** = "**community/media/scoops**."
- **The gap → we own "response / decision / literacy"**: what to **do** when it happens, how to **judge** true vs. false information, how society **should be governed**. This is the action layer and the cognition layer, which **no one currently occupies credibly.**

> The one-line difference: others help you **see** and **research** unidentified phenomena; we help you **understand** it, **judge** it, and know **what to do.**

## 5. Target-audience segmentation

1. **Science-curious / general pop-sci audience (the largest pool)** — want "reliable explanations," dislike pseudoscience and conspiracy theories. → content + membership.
2. **Rational enthusiasts / hardcore UAP circle** — want data, scales, first-hand sources. → tools + data + community.
3. **Educators (teachers / science museums / astronomy clubs)** — want ready-made courses, courseware, classroom activities. → B2B/edu licensing (high ticket).
4. **Media / journalists / brands** — want citable authoritative sources and data. → data/content licensing, sponsorship.
5. **The "preparedness" crowd** — want a "what do I do if it really happens" checklist. → tools + merch (**prudently; we don't sell fear**).
6. **The betting-oriented high-intent crowd** — follow prediction markets, love "odds/probabilities." → "evidence-rating/odds" content (no betting markets, information only).

## 6. Content pillars and signature features

### Content pillars (build trust)
- **Declassification tracking**: turn official materials from PURSUE/AARO/NASA, etc., into authoritative Chinese-language interpretations (already started, see the repo README).
- **Scientific scaffold**: SETI post-detection protocols, the Rio/San Marino scales, the METI debate, the "Who Speaks for Earth" governance question — explained as pop-sci anyone can follow.
- **Rumor checkup**: trace and evidence-rate hot rumors like "2027."

### Signature features (the differentiation others lack)

1. **The "Contact Response Protocol" interactive tool (First Contact Protocol)** ⭐ core
   - Rooted in **real frameworks**: the IAA *Declaration of Principles* (no unilateral response, coordinated via the UN), Rio Scale 2.0, the San Marino Scale, the Post-Detection Hub methodology.
   - Layered preparedness output: **individual/family → school/community → institution/government → all of humanity**, with each layer giving "if X happens, the authoritatively recommended action is Y, on the basis of Z (first-hand source)."
   - Emphasizes **verification first, no panic, no unilateral action, information literacy** — translating scientific consensus into an actionable checklist.

2. **The "rumor vs. evidence" rating system (Evidence Meter)** ⭐
   - Borrowing the **Rio Scale** approach, score each hot claim: credibility × impact → 0–10 points + an evidence tier (first-hand government document / peer-reviewed / media account / anonymous leak / pure hearsay).
   - "2027" and the like are flagged directly as "hearsay-tier, evidence = 0," with sourcing provided. **This is the key mechanism that converts a traffic topic into a credibility asset.**

3. **Scenario Simulator**
   - Interactive "what if...": signal detected / close-range sighting / official confirmation / physical contact... branching out the social, economic, and psychological responses, each conclusion tied to a scientific/historical basis (e.g., panic is actually rare; it's the information vacuum that's dangerous).

4. **Citizen observation + map (long-term, Form B)**
   - Standardized reporting + map aggregation + automatic scale-based rating; connected to the "response" layer, forming a data moat.

## 7. Credibility and scientific-governance mechanisms (the core of the moat)

- **Scientific advisory board**: invite scholars in astronomy/SETI/astrobiology/science communication/legal governance to lend their names (even lightly). Aim for Galileo's academic tone.
- **Public editorial standards**: source grading, correction policy, conflict-of-interest disclosure — operate like a news organization.
- **First-hand sources first**: every conclusion links to a `.gov/.edu/` or peer-reviewed source; second-hand accounts are clearly labeled.
- **Standardized scales**: all "credibility" judgments use the public Rio/San Marino methodology — **reviewable, not arbitrary.**
- **Ethics charter** (see §12): anti-panic, anti-pseudoscience, anti-fraud — written into the product DNA and published.

## 8. Monetization stack (ethical monetization, in phases)

> Principle: **build trust and traffic first, then monetize; prioritize models with "proven willingness to pay" (content/education) and treat "unproven" models (preparedness consumer goods) with caution.**

**Phase 1 (months 0–6, validation)**
- **Free newsletter + pop-sci short videos**: not directly profitable; the top of the funnel (acquisition).
- **Paid membership/subscription** (Ghost/Substack/Xiaobaotong): in-depth interpretation, the full protocol tool, distraction-free reading. Benchmark: niche newsletter free→paid conversion ~3–8%, ARPU ~$5–8/month.
- **Tips/crowdfunding** (Zhishi Xingqiu / Afdian / Buy Me a Coffee): low-threshold startup.

**Phase 2 (months 6–18, scaling)**
- **Online courses/workshops**: "How to tell real from fake UAP information," "school astronomy + critical-thinking course packs" — **strong willingness to pay for education, high ticket.**
- **B2B/edu licensing**: courseware, scale APIs, data dashboards licensed to schools/science museums/media. High ACV, low marginal cost.
- **Brand sponsorship/content partnerships**: science/tech/astronomy brands sponsoring the newsletter and podcast (**only those consistent with credibility**).

**Phase 3 (month 18+, the moat)**
- **Data products**: standardized sighting datasets licensed to researchers/media/insurers/drone-security B2B, etc.
- **IP/merch**: high-quality designed merch, books, documentary/podcast IP (**aesthetics-driven, not fear-driven**).
- **Membership community/offline events**: stargazing camps, salons, an annual conference.

> **Honest reminder**: this is a **slow business (content + community compounding)**, not an explosive SaaS. The first-year goal is "a credible brand + a few thousand paying members," not "a million in revenue." Model it as a media + education company, not a consumer app.

**Rough unit economics (illustrative, not a commitment)**: 50k monthly visitors → 5,000 free subscribers → 5% paid = 250 people × ¥40/month ≈ ¥10k/month to start; stacking 1–2 edu/B2B licensing contracts (¥50k–200k per contract per year) is the real profit driver. **The real leverage is in B2B/edu, not C-side tips.**

## 9. Technical architecture suggestions

- **Frontend/site**: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind; content via MDX/Headless CMS (Sanity/Contentful or Ghost). SEO and performance first (the topic acquires via search/social).
- **Interactive tools**: the protocol tool/rating/simulator built as React components + lightweight state machines; rules and bases stored as structured JSON (auditable, citable).
- **Data layer**: Postgres (Supabase) for sighting reports/ratings; PostGIS + MapLibre for the map.
- **AI usage (restrained, credible)**:
  - Use an LLM for **structuring materials, multilingual translation, draft summaries, clustering similar sightings**;
  - **Never** let AI "make up" conclusions or evidence — all outward-facing facts must link to a first-hand source; AI output goes through "human review + source verification."
  - Use the latest Claude models (e.g., Claude Opus 4.8 / Sonnet) for the content pipeline.
- **Payments**: globally use Stripe (subscriptions); in China use WeChat Pay / Zhishi Xingqiu / Xiaobaotong (see §10).
- **Scraping/data pipeline**: reuse the `uap_scraper` already written in this repo (pure standard library, egress-aware); once hosts are allowlisted, it can pull official materials automatically.

## 10. China vs. global: market and compliance localization (key risk)

**Core judgment: treat the "global English market" as the primary monetization ground, and treat "China" as a content/traffic and pop-sci brand ground — not the primary place to collect money.**

- **China content-compliance red lines**: "UFO/alien" as **pop-sci/entertainment** is viable; but framings like "**apocalypse/disaster prophecy**," "**the 2027 big event**," and "**government cover-up**" are easily judged as **rumor/superstition/disturbing social order**, risking throttling or banning of accounts.
  - Countermeasure: in Chinese channels, **only do "pop-sci + thought experiment + critical thinking"** as the persona; **downplay the "2027 prophecy"** and emphasize "how not to be swept along by rumors"; don't touch conspiracy theories, don't insinuate about officials.
- **China monetization channels**: Zhishi Xingqiu / Xiaobaotong (subscriptions), WeChat Channels / Bilibili / Xiaohongshu (traffic and ads), WeChat mini-programs (tools). **Cannot use Patreon/Stripe.**
- **Global channels**: English site + Substack/Ghost + Stripe + YouTube/X; lighter regulation, mature payment infrastructure, higher ARPU. **The protocol tool, data licensing, and courses target mainly the global English B/C side.**
- **Data compliance**: user reports include location/media → adapt to GDPR / China's PIPL: minimal collection, anonymization, explicit consent, deletability.

## 11. Sustainability and the moat

1. **Credibility brand** (hardest to copy) — accumulated through time, standards, advisors, and a correction record.
2. **Structured knowledge base + scale methodology** — citable, reusable by media/education again and again.
3. **Citizen-observation data network effects** (Form B) — more value the more people use it.
4. **Diversified revenue** — content/education/B2B/data/IP spread risk, not dependent on a single channel or platform.
5. **Cross-lingual** — bilingual CN/EN content assets covering two large markets.

---

## 12. Ethics and compliance charter (published on the site)

- **No panic-mongering**: every "what should you do" is keyed to "verify first, stay calm, don't act unilaterally."
- **No pseudoscience**: no claims unsupported by evidence; hearsay is clearly tiered.
- **No fraudulent monetization**: no products/services with false efficacy; no "countdown anxiety" to force purchases.
- **Source transparency + correctability**: public sources and a correction record.
- **Privacy first**: report data minimized, anonymized, deletable.
- **Political neutrality**: no insinuation, no conspiracy theories, no targeting of specific governments/ethnic groups.

## 13. Adversarial self-check (put the doubts out in the open)

| Doubt | Response / countermeasure |
|---|---|
| **Could it slide into pseudoscience/fear marketing?** | Use "evidence grading + first-hand sources + the ethics charter" as hard constraints; visually layer hearsay and fact; add "timeliness of corrections" to the KPIs. |
| **Could monetization become a scam / illegal?** | Cut all "efficacy-type" consumer goods; focus on content/education/B2B (proven willingness to pay); make ads/sponsorship compliant with disclosure; route payments through legitimate channels. |
| **Will people really pay for "alien preparedness"? Is demand overestimated?** | **Admit the uncertainty.** So in year one we **don't bet on the C-side "preparedness consumption,"** but on the proven-to-earn "in-depth content + education + B2B licensing"; use free tools to drive traffic and paid content/courses to monetize. |
| **Is there a real moat vs. Enigma/Galileo?** | Don't compete head-on (reporting/research); occupy the empty "response/literacy/decision" layer; the moat is the credibility brand + methodology + bilingualism + diversified revenue, not a single dataset. |
| **China regulatory risk?** | Content persona is "pop-sci/critical thinking," downplay prophecy, avoid conspiracy theories; put the main monetization battlefield in the global English market; the China side is mainly brand/traffic. |
| **What if the topic cools after "2027" passes?** | The hook is replaceable (there will always be new "dates/events"); the real asset is **evergreen scientific-literacy content + tools + community**, not tied to a single date. |

## 14. MVP and roadmap

**MVP (4–6 weeks, can start from this repo)**
1. A content site (Next.js) + 3–5 flagship articles (reuse the existing Chinese UAP interpretations).
2. **"Rumor vs. evidence" rating** debut: with "the 2027 rumor" as the sample case.
3. **"Contact Response Protocol" tool v0**: individual/family layer + scientific-basis links.
4. Free newsletter subscription + a paywall placeholder.
5. Reuse `uap_scraper` to automatically update official material sources (once hosts are allowlisted).

**90 days**: a stable update cadence (weekly) + fill out the school/institution layers of the protocol tool + launch paid membership + ramp up social channels (Bilibili/Xiaohongshu/X/YouTube).
**6–12 months**: launch the scenario simulator + the citizen-report map (B) + the first course / first edu license + bring in an advisory board.

**North-star metric**: credibility (number of citations by authoritative media/education).
**Supporting KPIs**: weekly update rate, free subscriber count, free→paid conversion, paid-member retention, tool completion rate, correction timeliness, number of B2B contracts.

**Financial model (conservative, illustrative)**:
- Month 6: ~5k free subscribers, ~200–400 paid, ¥10k–20k/month (mainly content membership).
- Month 12: ~20k–30k free subscribers, ~1k paid + 1–3 edu/B2B contracts, ¥50k–150k/month (B2B/courses become the mainstay).
- View it as a **compounding content + education company**: reaching "supports a small team" level in 18–24 months is a realistic goal.
